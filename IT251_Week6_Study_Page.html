How to use this week

Modules 1–4 are complete as of Week 5 — the written midterm (Week 8) and this week's review both cover exactly that scope, nothing more. Rather than re-teach any of it, this page points you back to each week's own study page, where the flashcards, quiz, and glossary you already used are still there and still searchable.

Best use of this week: don't just re-read — open each study page below, shuffle its flashcards, and retake its quiz cold. If a quiz question still trips you up, that's exactly the material to spend more time on before Week 8.

Worked example

How to actually study a "weak" module

Say Module 3 (Week 4) feels shaky. The efficient path isn't re-reading the whole page top to bottom:

  1. Open Week 4's quiz cold — no peeking at the page first.
  2. For every question you get wrong, jump straight to that term in the glossary (it's searchable).
  3. Reread just that entry, then retake the quiz — repeat until nothing misses.

This targets the actual gaps instead of re-reading material you already have cold.
