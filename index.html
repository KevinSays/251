Week-by-week index

Each week runs two sessions, numbered N.1 (lecture) and N.2 (lab), tracked separately — a week can have one filed and one still pending. Filed lecture pages open a full study page (concept diagrams, flashcards, a sample quiz, worked examples, glossary); filed lab pages open an interactive follow-along with step-by-step checklists. Weeks with no scheduled meeting are marked self-study rather than left blank.

note

Most weeks are still unfiled — that's not missing work, it's the semester not having happened yet. Each page gets built out the week of, or the week before, its class date.

about

How this site works

N.1 lecture pages are self-contained study guides: concept diagrams, flashcards, a sample quiz, worked examples, and a glossary. N.2 lab pages are interactive follow-alongs with step-by-step checklists your browser remembers as you check things off — nothing is submitted anywhere from this site itself. All of it runs as plain static HTML, no build step, no tracking.
